Fitbit Health Sync copies selected health metrics into Apple Health on this iPhone. Data is processed on-device. There is no account with us and no server storing your health data.

Connect with Google Health (the path going forward). Existing Fitbit connections keep working until Google turns down the Fitbit Web API in September 2026. Tokens do not transfer — reconnect with Google before then.

Metrics you can sync:
• Body weight
• Body fat percentage
• Steps
• Sleep
• Resting heart rate
• Active energy

Choose which metrics to write and how often to attempt background sync. iOS decides when background work actually runs.

Privacy policy: https://lefthandmagic.github.io/fitbit-health-sync/privacy
