# Organization

**Fit Health Sync** / **Fitbit Health Sync** is a product of **Praveen Murugesan** (Left Hand Magic), Amsterdam.

## Mission

Build a small, on-device tool that copies the user’s own Google Health (or legacy Fitbit) metrics into Apple Health. No account with us. No health-data server.

## Product

- App Store name: Fitbit Health Sync
- OAuth / brand name: Fit Health Sync by Praveen Murugesan
- Bundle ID: `com.praveenmurugesan.FitbitHealthSync`
- Purpose: read-only Google Health sync into Apple Health on this iPhone
